Counter-Strike 1.6 AI · Phase 3: RL aim training (aim_bc)

Pixels in, mouse & trigger out · fair-play rules (200 ms reaction delay at the end of the curriculum, 25 decisions/s, human turn speed) · offline only

Kill rate in training (rolling, last 200 rounds)

Yellow line = pass mark for the current stage. Dashed grey lines = switch-overs to a fixed setup.

Damage dealt per round (HP)

100 HP = one kill's worth. Rising damage usually comes before rising kills.

Firing (share of decisions with trigger held)

Too low = too passive; near 100% = spraying. Pros fire on ~7% of steps over a whole match (more during fights).

Randomness of decisions (0 = always the same, 1 = fully random)

Should fall slowly as it gets confident. A sudden rise back toward 1 means it is forgetting.

Exams (fixed tests on the newest snapshot, every ~30 min)

Each exam = 100 rounds with the same random seed, so results are comparable over time: the run's current stage, and the next stage (is it ready?). The exam is the trustworthy number; training stats are noisy.

StageWhere it is in the curriculum ladderMoving up; a step back means the last jump was too big
Rewards (training only): +1 kill, +0.01 per HP dealt, −0.5 for a round that runs out without a kill (spread over the round), small cost per shot, aim-shaping bonus for moving the crosshair toward the bot; once the bot shoots back: −1 death, −0.01 per HP lost. The agent itself only ever sees the screen.
